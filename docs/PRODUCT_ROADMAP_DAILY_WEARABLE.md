# Daily Wearable Product Roadmap

This roadmap records product-level follow-up beyond the current edge/cloud prototype.

## Near term

- Validate obstacle, head-height, and drop-off behavior with blind and low-vision participants under supervised conditions.
- Measure false alerts, missed hazards, speech interruption, and latency across different camera placements and lighting.
- Add guided setup for camera field of view, camera height, pitch, and indoor/outdoor thresholds.
- Review privacy defaults, retention controls, profile export/deletion, and consent for any image-based diagnostics.
- Verify command recognition and speech controls across supported microphones, languages, and operating systems.

## Next

- Evaluate tracking stability for non-COCO objects and target handoff while the wearer turns.
- Test battery, thermal, and frame-rate limits on intended wearable hardware.
- Add a user-controlled physical stop and recovery flow for camera or model failure.
- Provide accessible onboarding and a repeatable fit/calibration check.

## Before deployment

- Complete safety engineering, accessibility research, security review, and field validation.
- Define supported hardware and environmental operating limits.
- Document failure modes and provide a clear fallback to established mobility tools.
- Do not market prototype metrics as clinical or field-proven results.
